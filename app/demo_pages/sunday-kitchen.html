Easy Lemon Drizzle Cake

Prep 15 minutes · Bake 40 minutes · Serves 8

This is the cake I make every Sunday. It is quick, forgiving and always disappears within an hour.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a 2 lb loaf tin.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour and lemon zest, then spoon the batter into the tin.
  4. Bake for 40 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mix the lemon juice with the icing sugar and pour it over the warm cake.

Tip: leave the cake to cool completely before slicing. Store in an airtight tin for up to three days.
