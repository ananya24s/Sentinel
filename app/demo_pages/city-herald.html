City Council Approves New Bus Routes

By Dana Whitfield · Published 18 September 2026 · 3 min read

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve three new bus routes connecting the east side to the downtown transit hub, ending a campaign that residents have run for almost six years.

Under the plan, service on the new lines will begin in March, with weekday frequencies of one bus every fifteen minutes. The budget for the expansion was set at 4.2 million dollars over two years, paid for by a mix of state transit grants and a small increase in parking fees.

Councillor Ravi Menon said the decision showed that "public transport is a public promise." Critics argued that the money could have gone to road repairs, but the motion passed by seven votes to two.
